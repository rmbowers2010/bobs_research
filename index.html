Research focus

Genomes, communities, and practical analysis systems

My work connects wet-lab sequencing, computational genomics, and tools that make large research datasets easier to inspect, compare, and share.

01

Single-cell genomics

Production and analysis of single amplified genomes, including methods for improving genome recovery from individual microbial cells.

02

Metagenomes and isolate genomes

Comparative analyses, quality control, taxonomy, and genome-scale summaries for environmental and host-associated microbial datasets.

03

Reproducible research software

R, Python, Bash, HPC, cloud, containerized workflows, and Shiny apps built for collaborators who need to explore data directly.
